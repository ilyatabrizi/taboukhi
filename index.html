The house

The first eyewear house of Ilya Taboukhi — and it is only beginning.

TABOUKHI is the first eyewear brand established by Ilya Taboukhi. It starts from a plain belief: that something worn on the face, seen in every room and in every conversation, deserves the materials and the attention usually kept for the objects people lock away.

We are at the very start. There is no archive to lean on and no habit to follow; there is only a standard to set. This page exists for one reason: to find the people who will set it with us.

An invitation, not a storefront. Nothing here is for sale.

The work

Two things set the work apart.

Material

Precious by material.

TABOUKHI eyewear is made from precious and costly materials. We choose them because the result is an object of real and lasting worth, not the look of it. Light should have something worth landing on.

Commission

Personal by design.

For select clients, the house goes further. They can design and personalise their own eyewear with us: a pair conceived with one person, for one person, and made to their order. It asks for listening as much as for making.

Work of this kind is only ever as good as the people who do it.

The people

We are looking for the few who are exceptionally good at what they do.

TABOUKHI is assembling its founding team. We are interested in professionals at the top of their discipline: people with a record of serious work, the patience for detail, and the wish to build something from its first day. We would rather wait for the right person than fill a seat.
